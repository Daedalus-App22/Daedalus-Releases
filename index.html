Number Formatting

Apply finance-standard number formats across any selection instantly. Number, percentage, currency, and multiples formats. The common formats you need for every model, mapped to single keystrokes, and fully customizable to your own preferences. No ribbon, no formatting dialog, no interruption.

Color Cycling

Color-code hardcodes, formulas, cross-sheet links, and external references instantly. Industry standard font and cell fill colors out of the box or fully customizable to your own convention. Define your palette once, and Daedalus applies it with a single keystroke.

Formula Auditing

Trace, visualize, and audit formulas without leaving the keyboard. Surface inconsistencies across your model, trace precedents and dependents, and navigate formula logic fast. Built for the finance professional who needs to get in, understand the model, and move on fast.

Export to Presentations

Copy any selection to the clipboard with color-coded fonts automatically converted to black. Paste cleanly into PowerPoint or Word in whatever format you choose. Your source model stays intact. Colors, formulas, everything unchanged.
